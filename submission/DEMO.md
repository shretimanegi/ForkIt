## What the video should show

1. Brief introduction to the problem — why finding debris in raw sonar imagery
   is hard.
2. The proposed solution.
3. Main features and workflow: upload an XTF file, watch the job run, review the
   processed waterfall, the detection table and the map.
4. The actual working prototype — a real detection with its coordinates and
   measured dimensions.
5. Report export in JSON / CSV / GeoJSON.

Keep the video focused on the project and make sure reviewers can access it
without requesting permission.
